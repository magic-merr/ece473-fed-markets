Generated by `src/decomposition.py` on 2026-11-15, repo commit fb94863, `index/` at commit fb94863. Main price rule: `final`. Data through 2026-10-26. All values in index points (1 point = $1 on the $100 contract).

## Method

Take two consecutive days on which the index has a value. Between them, a portfolio holds w of the near meeting's contract and 1 - w of the far meeting's, at the first day's strike, with w from the first day. Its P&L is the **belief change**: how much fixed-meeting, fixed-strike probabilities moved. The **gap** is portfolio P&L minus the index change. Each interval is an announcement (an FOMC decision falls in it), a bracket roll (otherwise, the pair of meetings changes) or ordinary (neither). On an ordinary interval of k days the gap equals exactly -k times the term-structure slope (C_m2 - C_m1) / (d2 - d1) at its end, so it is the **roll-down**. The four parts add up to the summed index change:

index change = belief change + roll-down + bracket rolls + announcement resets

with each part signed as its contribution to the index. Held legs are priced at the index rule's price; if the rule has none on the second day, at the standing book as `index/futures.py` marks a hedge leg.

The Milestone 1 report used one-day pairs only (both calendar days with an index value, rule prices only). Under the 3-day freshness rule the index is often missing on the day before an FOMC decision, so one-day pairs would drop most rate-change days. The chained intervals keep them. The last row uses the Milestone 1 method and reproduces `index_m1/data/backtest_cut90.csv` and `backtest_hike90.csv`.
